Turns CAD into physical robot parts through CNC manufacturing. CNC does not care how elegant the model was. Conrad Challenge — iBlood

Team Lead

Led a team building a wearable graphene strain-sensor blood pressure monitor. Built a proof of concept, raised $500, and received the Conrad Innovator award. Blood pressure, it turns out, is less cooperative than aluminum.
