Looking Forward

Wants to study mechanical engineering, focused on assistive devices: parts a person holds, wears, or relies on every day, and that they can actually afford to own and repair.

The problem that keeps coming back is cost. Specialized wheelchair equipment for power soccer already existed; it was simply too expensive. Curious about that space between a clinical product and a version someone can build, fix, and use.

iBlood was a first step, not a finished device. Raised $500 and built a proof-of-concept blood-pressure sensor — one measurement, still rough. Later work should start the same way: a specific need, a hard constraint, and a prototype that has to function.
